        Sign in

        Sign in to sync your stars, "Not for me" choices, and saved filters across devices.

        Signing in is optional. We store only your Google email address and job preferences. They are kept in our database in the EU (Supabase), with a daily backup copy at GitHub in the United States. We never store your CV or a password, and we set no tracking cookies. (Supabase's own sign-in record also holds your Google name and photo until you delete your account.) By signing in, you agree to the Terms (opens in a new tab) and privacy notice (opens in a new tab).

        Delete your Misra account?

        This immediately and permanently deletes your account, your email address (), your saved filters, your stars, your "Not for me" choices, and any job alert settings and the record of your consent to them from our database. A copy remains in the daily backup until the backup is replaced, within 30 days; it is never put back into the live service.

        What this browser saved while you were signed out stays until you clear your browser's data. This cannot be undone.
