About Misra

Misra is a daily list of entry-level, student and product roles in Israel. It is for people looking for one of those roles.

Where the roles come from

Misra reads the roles from the companies' own career pages and job boards. It does not hire, and it does not recruit for the companies it lists.

Misra reads the career pages once a day. The bottom of the dashboard shows when they were last read. A role you see can already be closed, so check the employer's page before you apply.

What Misra does not do

  • It does not apply for you. Apply opens the employer's own page.
  • It does not rank roles by anything about you. You sort them by newest, company or title.
  • It shows the start of a posting, when the posting has text. The full posting is on the employer's page.
  • It lists roles in Israel unless you choose otherwise: the Abroad region in Filters shows roles elsewhere, and a switch also shows remote roles whose posting does not say where they are.
  • It does not cover every company. It can miss roles, and some listings can be out of date.

Coverage gaps

The Coverage gaps card, under the list, names the companies whose roles Misra knows it may be missing. It has two lists:

  • We choose not to include these companies: their site does not allow automated access, and Misra respects that.
  • We miss roles at these companies: Misra cannot find or read their open roles in Israel. Their roles may be missing or old.

The card lists only the gaps Misra knows about. To suggest a company, choose Add a company on the card. It needs no sign-in.

Sign in (optional)

The dashboard works without an account. Your stars, "Not for me" choices and filters are saved in your browser.

Sign in with Google to keep them in your account, so they follow you to your other devices. You can delete your account at any time with Delete my account. The privacy notice says what is kept and where.

Job alerts (optional)

Signed in, open Job alerts in the account menu to get new roles in Israel that match your filters by email.

  • Choose daily or weekly (Sundays).
  • Misra sends a confirmation email first. Nothing else is sent until you confirm.
  • An alert holds only roles that are new since you confirmed, and never the same role twice.
  • No alert is sent when there is nothing new.
  • The alert keeps the filters you set for it. Changing the dashboard filters later does not change it.
  • To stop, use the unsubscribe link in any alert (no sign-in needed), or End job alerts in the Job alerts dialog.

Connect your AI assistant (optional)

You can connect your own AI assistant, such as Claude or ChatGPT. It can search the roles and read one posting at a time. It cannot change anything and needs no sign-in. Connect your assistant has the steps and the limits.
